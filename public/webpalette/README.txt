WebPalette 23.0.0 — offline build

Double-click Website builder.html to describe a site and get it built, WebPalette.html to edit, or Components.html to browse every section and button (Button Studio.html and Section Studio.html are the engines behind them). Nothing to install; it runs in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
Projects are saved in the browser you open it with (IndexedDB). Use Export > Project backup to move them.

Works offline: templates, editing, palette engine, typography with the 27 bundled font families,
gradients, checks, import of HTML/ZIP files, and every export.
Needs a connection: Google Fonts outside the bundled set, and the reference screenshots in Inspiration.
Website builder, "like xyz.com": reading another website needs the local server (npm start in the full download). Offline, the builder uses its notes on 33 well-known sites (linear.app, stripe.com, airbnb.com…) and otherwise asks its five questions.
DesignNet weights and inference are bundled in this offline editor. No AI service or model download is needed. External Google fonts may fall back without internet.

Fonts in fonts/local are SIL Open Font License. Sample photos in assets/photos are for previews;
replace them before publishing a site.
